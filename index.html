Gargantua & Accretion Disks

A black hole's gravity is so immense that nothing can escape it. However, the glowing ring you see around it is called an Accretion Disk. It is made of superhot gas and matter spiraling into the black hole.

Gravitational Lensing

Notice how the disk appears to bend over the top and bottom of the black hole? That is a visual illusion caused by Gravitational Lensing. The black hole's massive gravity curves spacetime so intensely that light bends around it.

Time Dilation

Because extreme gravity curves spacetime, time moves significantly slower near the black hole compared to an observer further away. On Miller's Planet (which orbits Gargantua), 1 hour equals 7 years on Earth.
